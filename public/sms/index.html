Text-to-post SMS policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

What this is

The arynlynn.com phone number lets Aryn Davis post wine tasting notes to her website, Gen Z With A Glass, by text message. It’s a personal tool with exactly one user: Aryn.

Who receives messages

Only the site owner. Texts from any other number are ignored, and the number never replies to or messages anyone else. There is no subscriber list and no marketing.

How opt-in works

The owner opts in by texting the number herself, for example “wine help”, or a tasting note that starts with “wine:”. The number only ever replies to a text she has just sent. It never starts a conversation.

What messages you’ll get

  • A preview of the tasting note you sent
  • A confirmation with the post’s link after you reply YES (or a note that it was discarded after NO)
  • Help or error messages, like the text template or a value that needs fixing

Frequency: only in reply to your texts, usually two or three messages per post. Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP at any time to stop all messages, and START to opt back in. Reply HELP for help, or email hello@arynlynn.com.

Privacy

Phone numbers and message content are used only to publish posts to this website. Mobile information is not shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Texts are processed by Twilio (text messaging) and Netlify (hosting). A published tasting note becomes public on this website; the phone number it came from never does.

More detail is in the terms and conditions and the privacy policy.
